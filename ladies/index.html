First visit

New to walking football?

You do not need to know the rules or have played organised football before coming to HWFC.

Get in touch with us beforehand and we will make sure someone is there to greet you, introduce you to the group and help you get settled into the session. There is no separate trial-session format — you simply come along and join the session with support from the group.
