HWFC – Rules of the Game

Play safely. Play fairly. Enjoy the game.

  1. 1. No running

    Walk at all times, with or without the ball. One foot must remain in contact with the ground.

  2. 2. No physical contact

    No pushing, holding, barging or deliberate body contact.

  3. 3. Safe tackling

    No tackles from behind or from the side. No sliding tackles.

  4. 4. Ball height

    The ball must not exceed the designated height restriction.

  5. 5. No heading

    Do not deliberately head the ball.

  6. 6. Goal area

    Outfield players must stay outside the designated goalkeeper's area.

  7. 7. No offside

    The offside rule does not apply.

  8. 8. Free kicks

    Free kicks are indirect. Observe the required retreat distance.

  9. 9. Respect the referee

    Accept decisions without argument.

  10. 10. Spirit of the game

    Respect your opponents, play responsibly and remember that safety comes before winning.

It's walking football, not slow-running football!
