Pitchside quick guide

Rules of the Game

Play safely. Play fairly. Enjoy the game.

  1. Walk, don't run
    Keep at least one foot on the ground, on or off the ball.
  2. Avoid contact
    No pushing, holding, charging or barging.
  3. Tackle safely
    Challenge from the front; never slide tackle or challenge from behind or the side.
  4. Keep the ball low
    Stay within the height limit specified by the referee.
  5. No deliberate heading
    Use your feet, not your head.
  6. Respect the goal area
    Outfield players stay outside the goalkeeper's area; follow the goalkeeper restrictions.
  7. No offside
    Move into space without worrying about offside.
  8. Restarts
    Use kick-ins rather than throw-ins; follow the referee's instructions on free kicks, penalties and retreat distances.
  9. Respect the referee
    Accept decisions and avoid persistent infringements.
  10. Play for enjoyment
    Safety, fairness and respect come before winning.

Remember: It's walking football, not slow-running football!

Full laws and local rules

This is a quick guide, not a substitute for the applicable match laws. Check the referee's briefing for competition and session-specific rules, including ball height, goalkeeper restrictions, restarts and sanctions.
