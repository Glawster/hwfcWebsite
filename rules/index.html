HWFC – Rules of the Game

Play safely. Play fairly. Enjoy the game.

  1. 1. No running

    Walk at all times, with or without the ball. One foot must remain in contact with the ground. Running is penalised with an indirect free kick.

  2. 2. No physical contact

    No pushing, holding, barging or deliberate body contact.

  3. 3. Safe tackling

    No tackles from behind or from the side. No sliding tackles.

  4. 4. Ball height

    The ball must not travel above crossbar height. An indirect free kick is awarded to the opposing team for a height offence. A goalkeeper’s save that deflects the ball above the height limit is not itself a height offence.

  5. 5. No heading

    Do not deliberately head the ball.

  6. 6. Goal area

    Outfield players must stay outside the designated goalkeeper's area.

  7. 7. No offside

    The offside rule does not apply.

  8. 8. Free kicks

    All free kicks are indirect. Opponents must retreat at least 3 metres. Restart from the touchline with a kick-in, not a throw-in.

  9. 9. Respect and fair play

    Accept the referee’s decisions when present. Without a referee, acknowledge your own infringements, stop play when necessary and resolve disagreements calmly and fairly.

  10. 10. Spirit of the game

    Respect your opponents, play responsibly and remember that safety comes before winning.

It's walking football, not slow-running football!

Additional Social Session Rules

These apply to HWFC social sessions, including the Over 65s session.

  1. 1. Side-foot shots only

    All shots at goal must be taken with the inside of the foot. No laces, toe-pokes or other shooting techniques.

  2. 2. Shoot from the opposition half only

    Players must be in the opposition half to shoot at goal. No shots from your own half.

Full laws and local rules

This is a quick guide, not a substitute for the applicable match laws. These rules apply to HWFC social sessions, including self-refereed games. Players share responsibility for enforcing them. Formal competitions may have additional rules; consult the applicable published laws before playing.
