# HWFC Website – Home Page Copy and Wireframe

Status: Revised draft for review  
Scope: Phase 1 Home page only

## 1. Page Goal

The Home page should be a concise landing page rather than a duplicate of the detailed recruitment information on `/play/`.

Its job is to help a first-time visitor understand:

1. Who Hillsborough Walking Football Club is.
2. That walking football may be suitable for them.
3. That there are Men's and Ladies' opportunities.
4. That there are several weekly sessions.
5. Where to go next.

Detailed session descriptions, costs, venue addresses, what to bring and first-visit guidance belong on the **Play Walking Football** page.

# 3. Production Copy

## 3.1 Hero

### Heading

**Hillsborough Walking Football Club**

### Strapline

**Football for life.**

### Introductory copy

Friendly and competitive walking football for men and women in Hillsborough and the wider Lisburn area.

Whether you have played all your life, have not kicked a ball in years, or have never played before, you are welcome.

### Primary action

**Play Walking Football**

---

## 3.2 What Is Walking Football?

### Heading

**Football at a walking pace**

### Copy

Walking football keeps the passing, movement, teamwork and enjoyment of football while reducing the physical demands of the running game.

It can be social, competitive, or somewhere in between.

### Action

**Play Walking Football**

---

## 3.3 Who Can Play?

### Heading

**Could walking football be for you?**

### Copy

You do not need to be an experienced footballer or exceptionally fit.

**Men's walking football:** generally for players aged **50 and over**.  
**Ladies' walking football:** for women aged **35 and over**.

Beginners, returning players and experienced players are all welcome.

### Action

**Play Walking Football**

# 9. What the Home Page Should Not Repeat

The following belong on `/play/` and should not be duplicated in full on Home:

- Detailed explanation of walking football.
- Full session descriptions.
- Venue addresses.
- Session costs and membership fees.
- Footwear guidance.
- What to bring.
- Detailed first-visit process.
- Competition age-band explanation.
- Full contact-form guidance.

The Home page may reference these topics briefly and link to the detailed page.
