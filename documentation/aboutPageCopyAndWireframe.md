# HWFC Website – About Page Copy and Wireframe

Status: Draft for review  
Scope: Phase 1 About page

## 1. Page Goal

The About page should explain who Hillsborough Walking Football Club is, how it fits within the wider Hillsborough football family, what the club values, and which organisations or partners are relevant.

Suggested URL:

`/about/`

# 3. Production Copy

## 3.1 Page Introduction

### Heading

**About Hillsborough Walking Football Club**

### Copy

Hillsborough Walking Football Club brings together people who want to enjoy football, stay active and be part of a friendly club community.

We provide regular walking football opportunities for men and women, with sessions ranging from gentle and social through to more competitive football.

---

# 4. Who We Are

## Heading

**Football, fitness and friendship**

### Copy

HWFC is built around the idea that football can continue to be part of people's lives for many years.

Our members include experienced players, people returning to football after a long break, and people who are new to the game.

Alongside regular weekly sessions, the club can take part in fixtures, tournaments, social events and other activities.

---

# 5. Hillsborough Boys FC Relationship

## Heading

**Part of the Hillsborough football family**

### Provisional copy

> Hillsborough Walking Football Club is part of the wider Hillsborough Boys FC football family.

This wording is **provisional** and must be updated once the formal relationship wording is confirmed by the club.

The website should use the same Hillsborough badge / logo family as the Hillsborough Boys website.

---

# 6. What We Value

## Heading

**What matters to us**

The following themes are **proposed website values and remain subject to committee confirmation before publication**.

### Welcoming

New and returning players should feel comfortable coming along for the first time.

### Inclusive

The club should provide opportunities for different levels of experience and ability.

### Enjoyment

Football should remain enjoyable, social and something people look forward to.

### Community

HWFC is more than weekly sessions. Friendship, social activity and supporting each other are part of club life.

### Competition

Players who want competitive football should also have opportunities through fixtures, tournaments and age-group competitions.

If the committee does not wish to adopt these as explicit club values, this section should be simplified or omitted rather than presenting them as formally agreed statements.
