# HWFC Website – Men's Page Copy and Wireframe

Status: Draft for review  
Scope: Phase 1 Men's landing page

## 1. Page Goal

The Men's page should provide team-specific information without repeating the detailed recruitment content already owned by `/play/`.

Suggested URL:

`/mens/`

The page should help visitors:

1. Understand the Men's section of HWFC.
2. See which regular sessions are relevant.
3. Find fixtures, events, squad information and match reports.
4. Move easily to the Play page if they are considering joining.

## 3.1 Introduction

### Heading

**Men's Walking Football**

### Copy

HWFC provides regular walking football for men aged 50 and over, with sessions ranging from gentle and social football through to more competitive games.

Whether you are returning to football, already play regularly, or are trying walking football for the first time, there is a route into the club.

### Action

**Play Walking Football**

# 5. Competitive Football

## Heading

**Competitive walking football**

### Copy

HWFC takes part in matches, tournaments and other competitive walking-football events.

Teams may be entered in different age categories depending on the competition, including U55, U65 and U70.

These competition categories are separate from the normal eligibility for weekly club sessions.
