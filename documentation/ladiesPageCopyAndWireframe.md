# HWFC Website – Ladies' Page Copy and Wireframe

Status: Draft for review  
Scope: Phase 1 Ladies' landing page

## 1. Page Goal

The Ladies' page should provide team-specific information while also supporting the current priority of attracting more women to walking football.

Suggested URL:

`/ladies/`

The page should help visitors:

1. Understand that Ladies' walking football is an established part of HWFC.
2. Know that women aged 35 and over are welcome.
3. Know that previous football experience is not required.
4. See the regular Ladies' sessions.
5. Find fixtures, events, squad information and match reports.
6. Know how to make contact before coming along.

Detailed general recruitment information should remain on `/play/`.

# 3. Production Copy

## 3.1 Introduction

### Heading

**Ladies' Walking Football**

### Copy

HWFC offers walking football for women aged 35 and over, with regular opportunities for coaching, development and match play.

You do not need previous football experience to take part. Whether you have played before, are returning to football after a long break, or are completely new to the game, you are welcome.

### Primary action

**I'd Like to Try Walking Football**

# 4. Who Is It For?

## Heading

**Walking football for women aged 35+**

### Copy

Our Ladies' sessions are open to women aged 35 and over.

Previous football experience is not required. The aim is to provide a welcoming route into the game while also giving players opportunities to improve, play matches and take part in team events.

**Play Walking Football**

# 6. What to Expect

## Heading

**New to walking football?**

### Copy

You do not need to know the rules or have played organised football before coming to HWFC.

Get in touch with us beforehand and we will make sure someone is there to greet you, introduce you to the group and help you get settled into the session.

There is no separate trial-session format — you simply come along and join the session with support from the group.

### Action

**I'd Like to Try Walking Football**

# 11. Photography and Tone

The Ladies' page should use genuine HWFC Ladies' photography wherever possible.

The page should communicate:

- Football rather than generic exercise.
- Welcome and friendship.
- Learning and development.
- Match play and team participation.
- A range of experience levels.

Avoid imagery or wording that suggests someone must already be an experienced footballer to join.

---

# 12. What This Page Should Not Repeat

Keep the detailed general information on `/play/`:

- Full venue address.
- Costs and membership details.
- Detailed footwear guidance.
- General explanation of walking football.
- Full first-visit process.
- Full contact-form guidance.

The Ladies' page should give enough reassurance to encourage the visitor to continue, then link to the authoritative detailed information.
