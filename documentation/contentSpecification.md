# HWFC Website – Content Specification

Status: Phase 1 design baseline  
Purpose: Define the site purpose, information architecture, authoritative content ownership and implementation rules.

## 1. Site Purpose

The Hillsborough Walking Football Club website will be the authoritative public source for permanent HWFC information.

The website should:

- Help prospective players understand walking football and decide whether HWFC is suitable for them.
- Provide reliable information about sessions, venues, costs, contacts and how to get involved.
- Provide permanent homes for fixtures, events, match reports, squad profiles, photographs and club information.
- Act as the destination for links from Facebook, other social channels, partner websites, posters and QR codes.
- Support the wider HWFC marketing strategy without duplicating the role of social media.

### Editorial principle

**Social media tells people what is happening. The website tells people who we are and how to join us.**

Facebook remains HWFC's main community and regular-content channel.

---

## 2. Primary Audiences

### Prospective players

Need to know:

- What walking football is.
- Whether they are eligible.
- Whether previous football experience is required.
- When and where sessions take place.
- What sessions cost.
- How to come along for the first time.
- How to contact HWFC.

### Prospective Ladies' players

Need the same information, with clear evidence that Ladies' walking football is an established and important part of HWFC.

### Existing members

May use the site for:

- Fixtures and events.
- Match reports.
- Squad information.
- Club documents.
- Useful contacts and links.

### Family, friends and community

May be interested in:

- Match reports.
- Photographs.
- Events.
- Fundraising and community activity.

### External organisations

May need:

- Accurate club description.
- Club contact details.
- Partnership / affiliation information.
- Permanent URLs suitable for linking.

## 4. Content Ownership by Page

### Home

Purpose: concise landing page and signposting.

Home should contain:

- Short club introduction.
- Brief walking-football explanation.
- Basic age guidance.
- Sessions at a glance.
- Equal links to Men's and Ladies' sections.
- Brief first-visit reassurance.
- Facebook link.
- Clear calls to action.

Home should not duplicate detailed costs, addresses, footwear advice or the full first-visit process.

### Play

Purpose: authoritative recruitment and session-information page.

Play owns:

- What walking football is.
- Eligibility and age guidance.
- Full session descriptions.
- Venue details.
- Costs and membership.
- What to bring.
- First-visit process.
- Primary recruitment contact path.

Suggested permanent URL:

`/play/`

This should be the main destination for recruitment QR codes and general recruitment links.

### Men's

Purpose: Men's section information.

Owns:

- Men's / mixed sessions summary.
- Competitive football information.
- Fixtures and events.
- Squad content.
- Match reports.

Detailed joining information remains on `/play/`.

### Ladies'

Purpose: Ladies' section information and active recruitment.

Owns:

- Ladies' session summary.
- Women 35+ eligibility reminder.
- Coaching / development and match opportunities.
- Fixtures and events.
- Squad content.
- Match reports.

Detailed joining information remains on `/play/`.

### About

Purpose: explain who HWFC is, how it fits within the Hillsborough football family, and relevant club relationships.

Owns:

- Club overview.
- Hillsborough Boys FC relationship once confirmed.
- Agreed club values.
- Partners / affiliations once confirmed.
- Club-life overview.

### Contact

Purpose: club-controlled public contact route.

Owns:

- Website enquiry form.
- Official club email address.
- Facebook Messenger secondary route.
- Privacy / form behaviour.

## 6. Confirmed Eligibility

- Men's walking football: generally for players aged **50 and over**.
- Ladies' walking football: for women aged **35 and over**.

Competitive teams may be entered in age-banded competitions such as **U55, U65 and U70** as required.

These competition categories are not the normal eligibility rules for weekly club sessions.

- Members: £2 per session.
- Non-members: £3 per session.

## 8. First Visit

There is no special trial-session format.

Prospective players should contact HWFC beforehand and say which session interests them.

HWFC will make sure someone is there to:

- Greet the newcomer.
- Introduce them to the group.
- Help them get settled into the session.

## 9. Contact Model

HWFC will use club-controlled contact channels rather than publishing individual members' personal email addresses or mobile numbers.

Official email:

`info@hillsboroughwalkingfootball.com`

Primary public route:

```text
Website contact form
        ↓
info@hillsboroughwalkingfootball.com
        ↓
Authorised HWFC committee member(s)
```

Secondary route:

**Facebook Messenger**

Facebook page:

`https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100082995559544`

WhatsApp should only be promoted publicly if HWFC later establishes a dedicated club-controlled number.

### Contact-form enquiry categories

- I'd like to try walking football.
- Men's sessions.
- Ladies' sessions.
- Fixtures / visiting club.
- General enquiry.

Do not request unnecessary sensitive information at the initial enquiry stage.

## 13. Branding Direction

- Use the same Hillsborough badge / logo family as the Hillsborough Boys website.
- Use the existing HWFC / Hillsborough red-led direction.
- Dark grey and white as supporting colours.
- Carlito / Noto Sans typography where practical.
- Use genuine HWFC photography.
- Present players as active footballers rather than generic elderly-exercise imagery.
- Present Men's and Ladies' content as equal parts of the same club.
- Design mobile-first, especially session and contact information.
