Our club

About Hillsborough Walking Football Club

HWFC brings together people who want to enjoy football, stay active and be part of a friendly club community.

We provide regular walking football opportunities for men and women, with sessions ranging from gentle and social through to more competitive football.

Who we are

Football, fitness and friendship

HWFC is built around the idea that football can continue to be part of people's lives for many years.

Our members include experienced players, people returning to football after a long break, and people who are new to the game. Alongside regular weekly sessions, the club can take part in fixtures, tournaments, social events and other activities.

Club culture

What matters to us

These themes are proposed website values and remain subject to committee confirmation.

Welcoming

New and returning players should feel comfortable coming along for the first time.

Inclusive

The club should provide opportunities for different levels of experience and ability.

Enjoyment

Football should remain enjoyable, social and something people look forward to.

Competition

Players who want competitive football should have opportunities through fixtures, tournaments and age-group competitions.
